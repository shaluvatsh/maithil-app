# ADR 0009 — Bengali-first content model

Status: Accepted · 31 Jul 2026
**Amended by [ADR 0017](0017-english-by-default.md), 19 Sep 2026.** The content model below
stands unchanged — translations still live in their own tables, Bengali is still required at
seed time, and notification templates are still keyed by locale. What 0017 reverses is only
the *interface default*: the app no longer opens in the city's first language, it opens in
English and the reader chooses. The title of this ADR is kept as written for the record.

## Context
The PRD requires English + Hindi + launch-city language, with the purohit app fully usable in
the regional language. The handoff has no i18n at all: `rituals(name, description)` is
single-valued and cannot hold three languages. Notification templates have no locale key.

In Kolkata, Bengali is not the third language — it is the first. A 55-year-old purohit will use
the app in Bengali or not at all.

## Decision
- Locales: `bn` (primary), `hi`, `en`. Content tables have a `*_translations` sibling keyed
  `(entity_id, locale)`. `bn` is required at seed time; `hi` and `en` may fall back.
- UI strings via `i18next`, files under `packages/contracts/i18n/{bn,hi,en}.json`.
- Panchang terms (tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana names) ship as a **seeded static lookup**,
  since no provider gives us Bengali. Signed off by the consulting purohit.
- Money and dates render through a single formatter. Bengali numerals (০-৯) are a display
  toggle, off by default, on for the purohit app.

## Consequence, and it is the real cost
Someone must write 25 ritual descriptions and ~400 BOM line items in three languages. That is
content work, not engineering, and it blocks P0-4. Assign it in week 1 or it becomes the thing
that holds up the seed.

## Note on naming
In Bengal the practitioner is a **purohit** (পুরোহিত), not a "pandit". The product is called
The Maithil. Not a blocker, and the brand may be deliberate for a multi-city future — but all
*in-app* Bengali copy should say purohit, and it is worth knowing that the name reads as
slightly out-of-market to the first city's users. Flagged for the founder, not for engineering.
